- The Pipeline -

Viktor
The pipeline: Index built once offline (10,000 articles, 46,853 chunks). Query goes through keyword search (BM25) and semantic search in parallel, fused via RRF, top-K articles selected as context, then LLM generates cited answer.
Iteration 1 (First Prototype): BM25 only + flan-t5-small. No paper retrieved, LLM answered from general training only. Iteration 2 (Semantic Search): BM25 + FAISS (MiniLM) → RRF → Llama 8B. A paper retrieved but not very specific. Iteration 3 (Full Pipeline): BM25 + PubMedBERT + FAISS → RRF → Llama 70B. Relevant papers retrieved, specific, grounded, citable.

We used two evaluation approaches. First: PubMedQA benchmark with 1000 yes/no/maybe questions, automatic scoring. Second: 8 custom clinical questions with free-text answers, scored by two independent AI judges (ChatGPT and Claude) on a 1-5 scale.
Shows how well our dataset covers the test questions. Blue-grey bubbles are research topic clusters (COVID-19 20%, Oncology 21%, Epidemiology 20%, Dementia 10%, ICU 5%, Surgery 3%). Green dots show coverage zones: Well Covered (3 questions), Partial Match (1 question), Sparse Match (2 questions), Outside Corpus (2 questions). Questions outside the corpus are harder to answer well for every model.
Results: I2 (Hybrid search + 8B model) wins with 3.6/5. I1 (Baseline) scores 3.1/5. I3 (Big 70B model) only 3.2/5, barely better than baseline. I3 is superior only in well-covered zones; in sparse or outside-corpus zones it drops below baseline. What matters most: whether your corpus contains the answer, not which model you use.

Why do some patients with non-small cell lung cancer stop responding to checkpoint inhibitors?
→ Ask

Some patients with non-small cell lung cancer stop responding to checkpoint inhibitors due to the heterogeneity of tumor biology. Tumors with high PD-L1 expression are more likely to respond, but the presence of other mutations — such as EGFR, ALK, or ROS1 — can affect treatment efficacy. A second factor is the development of immune-related adverse events: a PD-L1 tumor proportion score of 1% or higher is associated with a higher risk of side effects, which can force treatment discontinuation. The exact mechanisms behind resistance are not yet fully understood, and further research into the tumor microenvironment is ongoing.
