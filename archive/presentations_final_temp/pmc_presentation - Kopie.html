The Core

Language Model + Retrieval — why both?

Language model alone

Strong at language generation, but trained on general corpora — not on current biomedical literature. Answers may sound plausible but cite no real source and can diverge from current evidence.

Language model + retrieval pipeline

At query time the model receives the most relevant passages retrieved from 44,393 PubMed articles. It synthesises an answer grounded exclusively in that retrieved evidence — with citable sources and measurable accuracy.

What the retrieval adds

Top-5 passages ranked by biomedical semantic similarity · Source article, year, and journal visible · Grounding measurable on held-out benchmark [68% on PubMedQA golden-69]

Known limits

Retrieval gap: −35 percentage points vs. direct paper access · Index covers Open Access articles only · Answers are not a substitute for clinical judgement

The language model receives the most relevant passages from the index before each response — and grounds its answer in citable evidence rather than general training.
